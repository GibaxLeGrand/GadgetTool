## GameBanana: where and what the rules ask (read 2026-11-20)

- Game page: [Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds, id 21640](https://gamebanana.com/games/21640);
  tools: <https://gamebanana.com/tools/games/21640> (add: `tools/add?gameid=21640`). Almost
  every tool there sits in **Other/Misc**. Closest neighbour:
  [Sonic Racing:CW Save Manager](https://gamebanana.com/tools/21025) (C#, GitHub source, 7
  screenshots, "Development State: Beta"); it backs saves up and restores them, it does not
  decrypt or edit them. Tool pages have tags (Operating System, Compiled Language,
  Development State, IDE Used), a homepage URL, an official download URL, a License tab.
- [Tools section rules](https://gamebanana.com/wikis/710): a title that says what the tool
  is; the right game and category; a real description; **credits for everyone involved,
  "including original artists, companies" and anyone with rights to what is used**; **at
  least one screenshot**; the archive holds only what the tool needs (plus readme), not
  damaged, not password-protected; nothing usable for illegal actions; no Steam password.
- [Site rules](https://gamebanana.com/rules) that matter here: R.8 Warez (circumventing a
  licence or payment: not what the save key does), R.29 Malware, **R.31 cheating software is
  not allowed** (GadgetTool must stay a preset editor: no unlocks, no currency, no stats,
  only plates the game allows), R.23 credits go in the Credits module, **R.34 AI policy**,
  **R.40 files that fail the site's analysis are refused** (an unsigned exe can trip it).
- [AI Generated Content Policy](https://gamebanana.com/wikis/2175): the submission's **AI
  Usage module** must say truthfully, per category (Art/Visuals, Code/Logic, Audio,
  Concept/Planning/Design, Submission Elements), how much AI did, from none to the majority;
  credit the AI service and link it; mainstream services (Claude is named) need no dataset
  credits; for code, dataset and input credits do not apply. **Human Contribution
  Requirement**: AI may help but must not be the sole contributor; "meaningful human
  contribution" includes programming, asset creation, refinement and integration;
  prompt-only development is not enough. Gibax's part here: the idea and every design
  decision, the plate look, the version and data rules, finding the save key and pak data
  with his OK, the in-game tests, reviewing and merging. He fills the module himself.
