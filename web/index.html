GadgetTool

Build, copy and share gadget presets for every local player, without opening the game.

The game's own save format is not supported yet: for now GadgetTool opens its sample save, with made-up gadgets.

Presets

    Click a gadget in the list to add it, or drag it onto the plate. Drag a 1-slot gadget onto the Super Slot. Click × or drag a gadget back to the list to remove it. Gadgets are arranged in the rows automatically.

    Preset A goes on A, B on B, and so on: the presets of the players you tick are replaced. Undo puts them back; nothing is written until you save.

    Share

    Anyone with GadgetTool can paste this code. It holds the gadgets only, not the player or the name.

    Save to a file

    A .gtpresets file keeps the gadgets and the names: keep it on the PC, or send it to someone who uses GadgetTool.
